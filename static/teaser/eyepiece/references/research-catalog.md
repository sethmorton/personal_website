# Instruments of discovery — pooled references

41 entries. Research date: 7 September 2026.

The horn represents exploration, invention and discovery. Its eventual transformation is not yet a resolved explanation of co-construction. This library is intended to support a concrete choice of material, motion and meaning.

## What the research suggests

- Use a matched structural handoff from the photographic horn to a drawing, borrowing the continuity principle from the Bass title sequence.
- Look at real deployment mechanisms before animating a speculative instrument. Connected ribs and hinges give movement a readable cause.
- Preserve one recognizable identity through material changes; Walking City and IBM Think are references for this problem.
- Three.js supplies surfaces, rigs, morph targets, line rendering and time control. Those capabilities enable the selected motion language; they do not choose its meaning.
- Compare three short rough studies before producing another full teaser: aligned handoff, mechanical opening, and transformation with retained identity.

## Review scope

Studio/archival descriptions and official technical documentation were read. Downloaded reference images and sampled Incredible Machine frames were visually inspected. Other films are collected watch links, not claimed full-film analyses. Two older NASA image URLs and an old training-clip link did not resolve; a separate NASA training photograph and Echo inflation images were retrieved through the NASA image API.
