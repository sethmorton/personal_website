Working direction, still open: the horn carries the spirit of exploration. Its transformation needs a recognizable identity, a clear movement rule, and connected construction.

Study the Bass handoff for continuity, Apollo mechanisms for unfolding, and Walking City for maintaining identity through change. The final instrument and its meaning remain design decisions; these references do not settle them.

Inside Incredible Machine

Selected frames sampled from the NFPF-linked preservation copy. These are viewing aids, not a shot-by-shot analysis of the complete film.

Three short studies to compare

1. Photograph → instrument drawing. Three seconds. Match the actual silhouette and seams, then reveal depth. Test whether the eye follows one object.

2. Instrument → articulated opening. Four seconds. A hinge leads, ribs follow, mesh gains tension. Test whether movement feels constructed and each part remains attached.

3. Opening → unfamiliar instrument. Four seconds. Preserve the throat, rim and one continuous carrier motion while the panels reshape one another. Test whether the horn remains recognizable and the change suggests invention.
