# Archival assets — co-construction teaser

15 newly sourced images, plus the existing NASA Holmdel horn photograph. Original files and indigo treatments are in `../plates/`; full metadata is in `../plates/manifest.json`. Photographs are period material (1948–1966), except the flight-data composite whose trace records a 1953 flight. No generated historical photographs.
